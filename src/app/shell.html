BeMerged — Player Guide

BeMerged is a survival-merge puzzle game built around one goal: upgrade all 24 unique modules to 5★ Ancestral before the board collapses under mines and bosses. Every turn is a mix of clearing space, upgrading pieces, dodging danger, and choosing the right loadout.

Your Goal

There are 24 uniques in the game. Each one has its own upgrade path, and you must raise all 24 to 5★ Ancestral to win.

If a unique gets destroyed or falls too far behind, your entire run slows down—so protecting them is part of the challenge.

Gameplay

Each turn you can:

  • Swap modules to make shatter combos (3+ matching modules).
  • Shatter to clear space and gain shards.
  • Long-press a module to enter merge mode and upgrade it.
  • Move uniques away from mines and bosses before they get trapped.
  • Change your equipped uniques (only when no boss is on screen).

The flow is: clear → merge → avoid hazards → repeat.

Shattering

A shatter combo is any horizontal or vertical match of three or more modules.

Shattering:

  • Clears space
  • Gives you shards
  • Causes the board to cascade
  • Creates room for new modules to spawn (including potential uniques)

Only shatter combos need to be aligned. Merges do not.

Merging

Merging is how you upgrade modules and progress your uniques.

Merge mode

  • Long-press any module to enter merge mode.
  • All modules that can merge with it are highlighted.
  • Tap any highlighted modules—anywhere on the board—to complete the merge.
  • Merges do not require adjacency or alignment.
  • Finish the merge by tapping the original module or the merge preview.

Merge rules

  • A merge uses one base module plus fodder modules.
  • Common modules cannot be bases.
  • Fodders must match the base’s mod type and rarity.
  • Regular modules stop at Legendary+.
  • Only uniques can advance into Mythic, Mythic+, and Ancestral stars.
  • Commons cannot be merged.

Merging is the engine that pushes your 24 uniques toward the finish line.

Bosses

Bosses appear every 100 moves, gain more HP each wave, and now apply pressure every single player move.

Boss behavior

  • Before the boss moves, it samples exactly 5 of the 8 surrounding tiles uniformly at random and decays each sampled module by 1 tier.
  • Any Common module touching the boss is destroyed immediately.
  • After decaying nearby tiles, the boss moves one step every player move along a deterministic shortest path toward the nearest unique.
  • Every 6 boss moves, it also bursts in radius 2 and decays affected modules by 2 tiers. Commons caught in the burst are removed.
  • You can still kite the boss, but its movement and damage cadence are now far more predictable.

Boss combo mode

  • Long-press the boss to enter combo mode.
  • Tap a module touching the boss to lock the combo type.
  • All modules touching the boss are auto-included in the combo.
  • Any mines adjacent to those touching modules are auto-included and their blast preview is folded into the displayed damage.
  • Tap more modules of the locked type anywhere on the board to extend the combo, then tap the boss again to fire it.
  • The preview damage is exact and deterministic for the current board state.

Mines

  • Mines still spawn over time, block their tile, and explode when their timer expires.
  • Double-tap a mine to trigger it instantly. There is no longer a 2-or-4 sacrifice step.
  • A triggered mine decays modules in its blast by 2 tiers. Commons are removed.
  • Mine explosions can contribute boss damage, and boss combo mode automatically includes linked mines next to touching boss modules.
  • SD now boosts mine spawn-rate control instead of changing boss timing.

Inventory

Inventory is your active loadout. Equipped uniques grant their effects, and a 5★ Ancestral unique now becomes a permanent effect when completed.

Equipping

  • Equipping a unique removes it from the board and places it safely in inventory.
  • You can equip one of each type: Cannon, Armor, Generator, Core.
  • To return a unique to the board, you must swap it with another unique of the same type that’s already on the board.
  • When a unique reaches 5★ Ancestral, its effect becomes permanent and that finished copy is removed from the board.

Restrictions

  • You cannot swap uniques while a boss is alive.
  • Any module adjacent to a boss or a mine cannot be moved, merged, or equipped.
  • If a unique becomes adjacent to a hazard, it is effectively trapped until the hazard moves or explodes.

Loadout timing and positioning are key to survival.

Shards & PF

Shards come from shattering modules.

You can:

  • Spend shards to increase the spawn chance of that module type.
  • Hold shards to power PF, which scales based on unspent shards.

You’re constantly choosing between immediate consistency and long-term scaling.

Unique Effects

Uniques define your build. Their effects grow stronger as they rise through Epic → Legendary → Mythic → Ancestral.

Cannons

  • AD - Rare+ modules deal +1 / +2 / +3 / +4 extra boss hits.
  • DP - 1 / 2.5 / 5 / 7.5 / 10% chance to instantly kill a boss on spawn.
  • BA - Each combo module has a 5 / 10 / 15 / 25% chance to deal 2× damage.
  • HB - Boss loses 1 / 2 / 3 / 5% max HP every 5 moves.
  • SR - Bosses spawn with 2.5 / 5 / 7.5 / 10% less HP.
  • AS - Boss combo damage gains 15 / 30 / 45 / 60%.

Armors

  • ACP - Commons touching merges/combos auto-shatter; each one adds +1 to boss combo.
  • SD - Stronger ranks improve mine spawn-rate control at 10 / 20 / 30 / 40, and mines still have a 15% reduced-radius roll.
  • NMP - Gain +3 / +5 / +7 / +9 extra moves before the boss begins destroying modules.
  • WR - Mines destroy 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 fewer surrounding modules.
  • SF - Boss takes 2 / 3 / 4 / 6 extra hits for each Rare module destroyed in its blast.
  • OA - Rare modules next to merges have 3 / 5 / 7 / 10% chance to promote to Rare+.

Generators

  • BHD - 2 / 4 / 6 / 8% chance for a unique Epic to spawn as Epic+.
  • SH - 2 / 4 / 6 / 8% chance to gain 2× shards from shattered modules.
  • PH - Rare/Epic drop rate increases by ×1 / ×1.5 / ×2 / ×2.5% each move while the boss is alive; resets on boss death.
  • GC - Each merge or shatter combo of 4+ reduces timer by 3 / 5 / 7 / 9s.
  • PF - Rare/Epic drop rate multiplied by log(1 / 2 / 3 / 4%) of unspent shards on hand.
  • RB - 3 / 5 / 7 / 10% chance for one module destroyed by boss or mines to promote instead.

Cores

  • OC - Modules next to the boss have 10% chance to deal +3 / +5 / +7 / +10 extra hits.
  • DC - Each boss combo adds +1 / +2 / +3 / +5 stacks toward the next combo.
  • HC - 5 / 10 / 15 / 20% chance for boss/mines to miss one non-common module in their blast.
  • MVN - Auto-shatter all common modules every 25 / 20 / 15 / 10 moves.
  • MH - Mines move toward the boss every 4 / 3 / 2 / 1 moves.
  • PC - Boss loses 1 / 2 / 3 / 4% max HP from the core combo effect.

Strategy Tips

  • Keep your uniques safe—losing one slows everything.
  • Equip the right uniques before a boss appears.
  • Move uniques away from hazards early; trapped modules can’t be swapped.
  • Use merge mode often—pull fodder from anywhere on the board.
  • Decide whether to spend shards or save them for PF scaling.
  • Kite bosses intentionally to control where destruction happens.

Leaderboard

  • Time, number of 5★ uniques, moves, merges, and shards all contribute to your leaderboard score on win or restart.
  • Using AI or Dev Tools during a run disqualifies it from leaderboard placement.
